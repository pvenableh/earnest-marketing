What both obey

  • Earnest is an adjective first. To be earnest is to mean it. The name is the positioning.
  • No in-house nouns as promises. No home, door, lens, pile, floor, rail, Boardroom. "Decide · Do · Know" appears only inside the column, as the UI shows it.
  • Every number is the demo workspace's, the same ones the live home uses. Every ask names a tool that ships; every "held" card is on the hold list.
  • The Voice Charter applies to the site. The biggest word on either page is "good".
  • The photographs are stock placeholders from Unsplash, tagged stock · replace, one src each to swap for the real shoot; the shot list is in the README.
  • The column is the real one: thread above, Do · Decide · Know chips, composer at the foot with the scope chip inside it (app Earnest/Column.vue, October).

Recommendation

  • Ship H's hero and beats as the live home. It makes the visitor want it, which is what Nike and BMW sold.
  • Keep G's creed as the six lines that open the About page and the launch post. It reads better as what the company believes than as a sales page.
  • If only one: H.
  • Either way, first: the three corrections below, on main, this week.
Wrong on the live site now, whichever concept ships

The Boardroom is sold in the Studio plan, the compare lead and a feature page. It was removed in the app's September rethink, with the Command Center, Focus mode and Mirror.

Plan names are Solo · Studio · Agency on the site and Solo · Team · Business in the app since 6 October. Same keys, same prices.

The who is "agencies, studios and freelancers". The app now signs up four shapes: agency, firm, practice, shop, each with its own words and modules.
