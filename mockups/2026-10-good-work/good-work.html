Good questions, straight answers.

What do I actually see when I open it?

One screen. A greeting with an honest read of the day, four numbers, and then what needs a decision, what's one tap, and what you only need to know. Earnest sits in a column on the right of every screen: the conversation above, the suggestions, and the composer at the foot.

There's a page for that. This one is the idea; earnest.guru/features is the literal list: every app, every floor, every verb Earnest can run, with a screenshot of each. Each section above links to its part of it.

Will it send emails or move money on its own?

No. Email, invoices, payments, meetings and campaign launches always wait for your tap. Small reversible things like a task or a ticket can run on their own if you switch that on, with an audit trail and an Undo.

Can my clients use it?

If you switch it on. In their portal Earnest answers as your studio and sees only what that client can already open. It can log a request, leave a note or book time with you. Nothing else.

Who can see my data?

Only the members you invite. Your workspace is isolated from every other organization, we never sell your data, and Earnest reads it only to produce your own results. It runs on Anthropic's Claude, on no-training terms. From your calendar it sees open times, never event details.
