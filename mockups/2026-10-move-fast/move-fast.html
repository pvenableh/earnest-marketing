The idea

Two halves, one sentence.

The line isn’t a slogan on top of the product. It describes how the product works, and that’s why it’s safe to say loudly. The speed is Earnest’s; the yes is yours. It also replaces “hustle”: it keeps the energy without the grind.

Earnest’s half

Move fast.

Reads the day, drafts the email, finds the time, writes the invoice, redrafts the post. One plain sentence in, a finished card out. On screen this part is always sped up, cut on the beat.

Your half

Mean it.

Email, invoices, payments and meetings wait for your tap. That’s a rule in the app (shared/ai-autonomy.ts), not a setting. On screen this part is never sped up: the frame holds, and the tap plays in real time.

Storyboard

Nine shots, twenty-three seconds.

Click a shot to jump the animatic to it. To make a :15, drop cuts 4 and 5 and halve each cut to two beats. For a :06 bumper, use the open, “Move fast.”, “Mean it.” and the end card. Every number comes from the homepage’s claim map (app/data/good-work.ts): the seeded demo workspace, with the $6,200 milestone marked as illustrative.

Motion grammar

Rules for anything that moves.

Use these for the site, the film, social cuts and anything later. Each one is in the animatic’s code, so this page is the working example.

  1. Earnest’s work is sped up. Your tap never is.Before every held action the frame holds for 400ms, the background slows to near-still, and a “Real time · your tap” tag appears. This is the visual form of “Mean it.” Never show a send, a payment or a meeting going out without it.
  2. Cut on the beat.120 bpm, so a beat is 500ms. In the :23 a cut lasts four beats (2s). In a :15 it lasts two. Transitions are a 140ms whip with motion blur. Never use a dissolve inside the montage.
  3. One verb per cut, said to the person.Know it. Book it. Chase it. Say it like you. Bill it. Use the customer’s own verb, never a product noun (no “Home”, no “column”, no “lanes”).
  4. Every fast run ends in stillness.The montage always ramps down (background 3× → 0.35× over 1.8s, eased) into a held photograph of people. Never end on speed. The live “Steady.” section is this stillness.
  5. Each cut runs in the same order.Ask types in (≈28ms per character) → reply snaps in, staggered 100ms → card → tap → green ✓ stamp. The ✓ copy says what really happened: “Sent, after you said so”, “Scheduled · not published”.
  6. Speed shows in the edit, not in numbers.Don’t show clocks, “in seconds” or “10×”. We can’t back a time-saved figure yet, so the pace of the cuts carries the claim.
  7. Type is the hero.Proxima Light, tight tracking (−.04em), huge. The only colour is the blue full stop and “Mean it.” Bodoni appears only in the wordmark.
  8. Footage is people working, not skies.The office loop (Mixkit 914) carries the montage and a person across the table carries the stillness. No sunrises, no oceans: those read “heavenly” (your note, 9 Oct).
Language bank

What it says, and what it won’t.

Move fast. Mean it.

Campaign line. Signed off, every time, with Earnest. Do good work.

  • Nothing goes out without you.The proof line under “Mean it.” True for email, invoices, payments and meetings.
  • It drafts. You decide.The mechanism in four words. Good for the hero lede or the FAQ.
  • Fast hands. Your say.For social, short.
  • Know it. Book it. Chase it. Say it like you. Bill it.The montage verbs. They also work as eyebrows on the homepage beats.
  • Good work moves fast — and means it.A possible turn for the creed, in place of or after “It needs a better Tuesday.”
  • Fast is never careless.Heading for the “What it never does” list.

Retired or off-limits

  • Imagine the hustle with ease“Imagine” makes it hypothetical, and “hustle” means grind. “Move fast” keeps the energy.
  • Not faster workNow in the live hero lede, and it contradicts the campaign. See the hero row below.
  • Autopilot · set and forgetFalse: the held list exists.
  • In seconds · 10× · save X hoursNo measured figure to back it yet.
  • #1 anythingEarn trust by being right, not by being loud.

Build path

Phase 2 · engine

Montage.vue

Port this page’s timeline (data-at / data-type / data-tap) into a component driven by scroll or by clock, reusing GoodWork/Column.vue. It takes the parked scrub’s place.

Phase 3 · page

The rhythm

Micro-cuts on the beat columns, the Steady speed ramp, the kept cards on the beat, and a hero loop of four asks.

Phase 4 · film

The spot

Record this stage to MP4 with Playwright at 1080p in both aspects, add a real sound bed, and post it. Then a :15 and a :06.

Decisions for you

  1. Hero headline: “Move fast. Mean it.” or keep “Do good work.”?

    Recommendation: “Move fast. Mean it.” as the H1, with “Do good work.” as the sign-off on every ending, the way a campaign line sits under a brand line. The alternative is to keep the H1 and use “Move fast. Mean it.” as the lede.

  2. Is the montage built from HTML or from re-recorded app video?

    Recommendation: HTML. It’s the column you already ship, so it can’t disagree with the page, and the re-record plan’s three open decisions stop blocking the homepage.

  3. Is the film made?

    The animatic is enough to brief an editor, or to record directly. Sound would be a dry percussion bed at 120 bpm that drops out at “Mean it.” Turn on the click track above to hear the tempo.
