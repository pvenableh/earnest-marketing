Straight answers.

What can my client’s Earnest see?

Exactly what that client can already open in their portal: their projects, requests, approval boards, invoices, proposals, contracts and meetings. Not your notes, your hours, your rates, your margins, your pipeline or any other client. Ask it for any of those and it says it cannot see that.

What can it do for them?

Three things, each one a card they tap: log a request, leave a note on a project or request, book time with your team. It cannot approve work, pay an invoice or sign anything — those stay the client’s own taps, in the portal.

Who pays for it?

You do, out of your plan’s monthly tokens, which is why it is a switch you flip rather than something every client gets. Flip it on for the clients who would use it.
