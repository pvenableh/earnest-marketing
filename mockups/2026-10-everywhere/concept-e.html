Straight answers.

What happens between “say it” and “done”?

Earnest writes a card: what it will create or change, every field filled from what it already knows. You approve, adjust or skip. Only then is anything written — as a normal record in the normal place, with “proposed by Earnest” on it.

Will it send emails or move money on its own?

No. Anything that reaches a client or touches money is held for your tap, always. Low-stakes, reversible work can run on its own once you dial that in, with a full audit trail.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

Earnest runs on a real language model, Anthropic’s Claude, but never from a blank prompt: it starts from your clients, your work, your money and your brand voice, and it can act in the app rather than hand you text to paste. Your data is never used to train the model.
