Straight answers.

How does it know which floor I’m on?

The floor is part of the address. Every floor of every app has its own sentence, written in that floor’s verbs, and that sentence travels with every ask. Open a record and the record’s own context goes instead. The chip above the composer shows you exactly what Earnest was told.

Is it a different Earnest in every app?

No. One Earnest, one memory, one conversation per app. Switching floors changes what it suggests and what it knows about the screen, not who you are talking to.

Will it send emails or move money on its own?

No. Earnest drafts, ranks and proposes. Anything that reaches a client or touches money waits for your tap, and approving creative work is the client’s tap, not Earnest’s.
