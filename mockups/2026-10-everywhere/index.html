What is new since September

  • The floor is part of the address. Every floor of every app has a one-sentence focus in its own verbs, sent with every ask, and three rows (Do · Decide · Know) above the composer. The chip reads MONEY · Invoices until a record is open, then INVOICE · INV-….
  • Four record types Earnest could not see: expenses, payments, appointments and approval boards — each with its own rows and server context.
  • Four new verbs: record an expense, record a payment, create a lead, add a contact. Every one proposes a card; you tap. Approving creative work is deliberately not a verb.
  • The reply shape: a bold line, up to four facts with the number first, the ask — under forty words — and a separate spoken line for “read replies aloud”.
  • One bar on the phone: below the desktop the rail folds into the composer; the apps open from a grid button; the waiting count sits in the pill.
  • Earnest for clients: an opt-in switch per studio (Organization → AI & Tokens, off by default, tokens bill the studio). It answers as the studio, sees only what the client can open, and can log a request, leave a note or book time. The leak probe refuses.
  • The portal has floors now too: Home · Work · Requests · Approvals · Schedule · Money, with three rows each.

How to read them

  • Every row and sentence is the app’s own string, copied from the awareness and prompt tables on main on 1 October. They live once, in shared.js, and all three concepts draw from it.
  • Every number is the demo workspace’s — $12k out across 6 invoices, $9,400 of it past 90 days at Meridian Law, 22 things today, Helios West 30 days late, $286k pipeline — the same figures the live home uses. The $45 Figma charge and the $350 check are the two illustrative ones (the E6 smoke run used $12 and $350).
  • The bars are coded, not captured. The September screenshots predate the floor chip and the lane rows, so the mockups draw the bar from the app’s strings instead of photographing it. A capture run against the live demo would let the real thing stand in.
  • Looks are shared. One token set per look × mode in shared.css, switched on <html data-look data-mode> exactly as the app does. #paper-dark and the like deep-link.
  • Pricing, FAQ, CTA and Hue’s footer are the September home’s, so any of the three ports into SellSheetHome.vue without touching landing.ts’s plans or compare rows.

Recommendation

Ship D’s hero on the live home, with E’s verb catalogue as its second section and F’s client split as its third.

The walk is the one thing a competitor cannot screenshot their way past, and it is cheap to port: the data is already a table. E’s catalogue answers the “but what does it actually do” question that “AI” alone never answers, and marks the guardrail on every row rather than once. F’s two-sided invoice is the strongest single picture of the portal, and the Tuesday is the sales deck and the launch post. Keep F whole for the agency-persona landing.
