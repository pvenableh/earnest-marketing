Straight answers.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

Earnest runs on a real language model, Anthropic’s Claude, but never from a blank prompt. It starts from your organization: your clients, your work, your money, your brand voice. And your data is never used to train the model.

What happens when it doesn’t have enough context?

It stops and asks. If it is thin on your brand or a client’s voice, it tells you what is missing instead of guessing.

Will it send emails or move money on its own?

No. Earnest drafts, ranks and proposes. Anything that reaches a client or touches money waits for your tap.
