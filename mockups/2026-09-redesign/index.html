What changed from A and B

  • Hero from A, shorter. One eyebrow, the headline, one sentence, two buttons, and the Focus demo. The lede went from 45 words to 22.
  • Switcher from B, right under the hero. Six tabs, six September screens, an Earnest chip per app. It replaces A’s Context Broker diagram and A’s six-app strip, and it carries the grounding claim with one sentence: every screen feeds the same memory.
  • Brand kept from A, cut to three lines. Profile card plus three drafts. No bullet list.
  • AI Actions kept from A, bullets removed. The proposed-change diff and the guardrail sentence do the work.
  • Pricing from B. The capacity ladder is shorter than three plan cards and says the same thing.
  • FAQ down to three. ChatGPT, missing context, and “will it send on its own”.
  • Wording. “No-training terms” became “your data is never used to train the model”, here and in the three original concepts.
  • Footer. Hue’s own footer pattern, centred and small: “created by” with the Hue logo (pink on hover), one line saying Earnest is made, sold and billed by Hue and appears as HUE STUDIOS on the card statement, which is the Stripe descriptor, and “© 2026 Hue” in tracked caps. Hue is the legal name, so “Hue Studios” is gone from every page here. The three concepts carry the same footer.

How the looks work

  • One page, one token set per look and mode, switched with data-look and data-mode on the root, which is how the app itself does it. Six combinations, nothing duplicated.
  • Glass is the default and opens dark: navy ground, sky-blue accent, light Proxima headings, glass cards, the wave field. Its light mode is a cool white with the same blue.
  • Paper opens light: linen, oxide accent, Bodoni headings, square corners, warm shadows. Dark mode is ink-on-dark-linen with a warmer oxide. The home and Money screenshots swap to their Paper captures.
  • Clean opens light: white, one blue, DIN condensed caps, borders instead of shadows. Dark mode is near-black with the same blue lifted. The home screenshot swaps to its Clean capture.
  • Deep links. #glass, #paper and #clean open a look in its default mode; #paper-dark and the like set both. The choice is remembered per browser.
  • Gap. Only the home and Money screens were captured in Paper and Clean, so the other switcher tabs show Glass screenshots in every look. A capture run with the other looks would close it.
