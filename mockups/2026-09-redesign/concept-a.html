Good questions, straight answers.

How is this different from ChatGPT or a generic AI assistant?

Earnest runs on a real large language model, Anthropic’s Claude on no-training terms, but it does not start from a blank prompt. It starts from your organization: your clients, your work, your money, your brand voice. One door, and it already knows where you are standing when you open it.

What happens when Earnest doesn’t have enough context?

It stops and asks. If it is thin on your brand, your goals or a client’s voice, it tells you what is missing rather than filling the gap with something plausible.

Will Earnest send emails or move money on its own?

No. Earnest drafts, ranks and proposes. Anything that reaches a client or touches money waits for your tap, and the autonomy ring on the door shows exactly what it may do without you.

Who can see my data?

Your organization, scoped by the roles you set. The model is called on no-training terms; your data never trains anyone’s model.
